Choosing between daily flexibility and fixed data

Mobile data disappears through ordinary actions: map refreshes, ride confirmations, translation, ticket screens, restaurant searches, and messages to the people waiting for you. For price checks and roaming savings, keep large updates, backups, and streaming on Wi-Fi. If a laptop hotspot or family sharing is part of the trip, choose more daily headroom than a maps-only traveler would need.

A useful way to estimate is to list the apps you will rely on and ask which of them you would miss most. Navigation and ride apps run for hours on travel days, while messaging barely registers.

Sharing a connection changes the arithmetic. Every device connected through your phone draws on the same allowance, including operating system updates and sync you did not ask for. Whether sharing is allowed at all depends on the plan's hotspot terms, which are listed on the plan page.

Where savings, flexibility, and convenience meet

This section keeps the focus on price checks and roaming savings for Colombia. Before choosing, picture the exact moments when being offline would be annoying: arrival, transport, check-in, route changes, or a payment screen. The right eSIM is the one that covers those moments without making you overbuy for days when hotel Wi-Fi will do most of the work.

Keep your home SIM in the phone for calls and bank messages, and let the eSIM carry the data. Two lines in one phone is normal, and the settings that matter are which line handles mobile data and whether roaming is on for the eSIM line.

Plan the installation for a day or two before you fly. That is early enough to sort out a problem, and late enough that the QR email is still easy to find.

The Colombia plans

As listed when these notes were last updated. The plan page keeps the current prices and terms, and any plan can be topped up or extended while it runs.

The list for Colombia has 12 plans: 4 priced per day and 8 with a fixed allowance. Daily plans run from 2.99 to 9.69 USD, with allowances from 500MB to 3GB per day. Fixed plans run from 2.99 to 66.99 USD, with validity of 7, 15 or 30 days.

Colombia eSIM 20GB 30 Days is both the longest fixed plan and the cheapest per gigabyte. It works out to about 3.35 USD per GB and 2.23 USD per day over 30 days.

Validity comes in steps, and trips do not. A trip of 8 days is one day longer than the 7-day plans, so the next fit is the 15-day plans. Counting arrival and departure days decides which step you need.

On the daily side, ten days at the smallest allowance (500MB per day) comes to 29.90 USD, and ten days at the largest (3GB per day) to 96.90 USD, if a daily plan is bought for each day.

A few questions

When should I install it?

At home, on good Wi-Fi, a day or so before leaving.

What is the least a Colombia plan costs here?

2.99 USD, for Colombia eSIM 500MB/Day.

How does the eSIM arrive?

By email, with a QR code and instructions. Keep the email until you are home.

How many plans are there to choose from?

12. 4 reset daily and 8 hold a fixed amount.

Can the home SIM stay in the phone?

Yes. Keep it for calls and messages and use the eSIM for data.

Does the phone need anything special?

It needs eSIM support and must be unlocked by your home carrier.
