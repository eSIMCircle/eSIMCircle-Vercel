Landing in Sri Lanka with data, for a short or long stay

Whether you stay a week or a season in Sri Lanka, it helps to land with the phone already online.

Published May 3, 2025 · Updated August 4, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Choosing between daily flexibility and fixed data

Mobile data disappears through ordinary actions: map refreshes, ride confirmations, translation, ticket screens, restaurant searches, and messages to the people waiting for you. For QR setup, activation, and phone settings, keep large updates, backups, and streaming on Wi-Fi. If a laptop hotspot or family sharing is part of the trip, choose more daily headroom than a maps-only traveler would need.

If you cannot decide between two sizes, take the smaller one and keep the option to top up. Every plan can be topped up with more data or extended with more days while it is active, so a first guess is allowed to be wrong.

If you will share your connection on most days, a larger or daily allowance fits better than a small fixed one. If sharing is an occasional need, a smaller plan with room to top up is usually enough.

Build the data plan around the real route

Build the plan around the first day that can go wrong. In Sri Lanka, that may be landing late, finding transport, checking into a hotel, or moving between stops before you have trusted Wi-Fi. A plan that covers those awkward hours is usually better than one that only looks good on a price table. For QR setup, activation, and phone settings, count the travel days from arrival time to the last transfer, not just the nights in the destination.

Arrival day also tends to be the heaviest data day of the trip. Navigation, ride apps and hotel messages all arrive together, before you have found Wi-Fi, so size the plan for that day first.

An eSIM is not a substitute for preparation. Save the address of your first stop, your tickets and an offline map before you leave, so a gap in signal costs minutes and not the afternoon.

The Sri Lanka plans

As listed when these notes were last updated. The plan page keeps the current prices and terms, and any plan can be topped up or extended while it runs.

The list for Sri Lanka has 15 plans: 6 priced per day and 9 with a fixed allowance. Daily plans run from 2.19 to 8.79 USD, with allowances from 500MB to 10GB per day. Fixed plans run from 2.55 to 87.99 USD, with validity of 7, 15, 30 or 180 days.

Among the fixed plans, Sri Lanka eSIM 20GB 30 Days is the cheapest per gigabyte, at about 1.48 USD per GB. The longest, Sri Lanka eSIM 50GB 180 Days, works out to about 0.49 USD per day over 180 days.

Validity comes in steps, and trips do not. A trip of 8 days is one day longer than the 7-day plans, so the next fit is the 15-day plans. Counting arrival and departure days decides which step you need.

On the daily side, ten days at the smallest allowance (500MB per day) comes to 21.90 USD, and ten days at the largest (10GB per day) to 85.90 USD, if a daily plan is bought for each day.
