How to estimate usage without overbuying

Plan selection notes for this article scenario

Read the plan cards as a fit check, not a scoreboard. Fixed-data plans are useful when your schedule and data habits are predictable. Daily or flexible plans are better when some days may be heavier than others. Compare Central African Republic eSIM options by validity, data ceiling, hotspot expectations, and setup timing before price becomes the deciding factor.

Build the data plan around the real route

Build the plan around the first day that can go wrong. In Central African Republic, that may be landing late, finding transport, checking into a hotel, or moving between stops before you have trusted Wi-Fi. A plan that covers those awkward hours is usually better than one that only looks good on a price table.
