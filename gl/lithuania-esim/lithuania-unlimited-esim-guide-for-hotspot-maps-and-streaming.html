Lithuania data for hotspots, maps and an evening film

Maps by day, a shared hotspot in the afternoon, a film in the evening. That kind of Lithuania trip needs a roomier plan.

Published June 18, 2025 · Updated September 25, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Choosing between daily flexibility and fixed data

Mobile data disappears through ordinary actions: map refreshes, ride confirmations, translation, ticket screens, restaurant searches, and messages to the people waiting for you. For price checks and roaming savings, keep large updates, backups, and streaming on Wi-Fi. If a laptop hotspot or family sharing is part of the trip, choose more daily headroom than a maps-only traveler would need.

Size the data from what you do, not from how long you stay. Maps, messaging and browsing use little. Video calls, uploads and hotspot sharing use a lot, and they tend to fall on the same days. Start from the busiest day you can picture and check that the plan covers it.

Sharing a connection changes the arithmetic. Every device connected through your phone draws on the same allowance, including operating system updates and sync you did not ask for. Whether sharing is allowed at all depends on the plan's hotspot terms, which are listed on the plan page.

Where savings, flexibility, and convenience meet

This section keeps the focus on price checks and roaming savings for Lithuania. Before choosing, picture the exact moments when being offline would be annoying: arrival, transport, check-in, route changes, or a payment screen. The right eSIM is the one that covers those moments without making you overbuy for days when hotel Wi-Fi will do most of the work.

Keep your home SIM in the phone for calls and bank messages, and let the eSIM carry the data. Two lines in one phone is normal, and the settings that matter are which line handles mobile data and whether roaming is on for the eSIM line.

A failed installation is cheap to fix at home and expensive in an arrivals hall. Do it while Wi-Fi is stable, keep the QR email until the trip is over, and leave the profile on the phone unless support tells you to remove it.

The Lithuania plans

As listed when these notes were last updated. The plan page keeps the current prices and terms, and any plan can be topped up or extended while it runs.

The list for Lithuania has 9 plans: 2 priced per day and 7 with a fixed allowance. Daily plans run from 2.29 to 2.99 USD, with allowances from 1GB to 2GB per day. Fixed plans run from 3.55 to 66.99 USD, with validity of 7, 15, 30 or 180 days.

Lithuania eSIM 50GB 180 Days is both the longest fixed plan and the cheapest per gigabyte. It works out to about 1.34 USD per GB and 0.37 USD per day over 180 days.

Validity comes in steps, and trips do not. A trip of 8 days is one day longer than the 7-day plans, so the next fit is the 15-day plans. Counting arrival and departure days decides which step you need.

On the daily side, ten days at the smallest allowance (1GB per day) comes to 22.90 USD, and ten days at the largest (2GB per day) to 29.90 USD, if a daily plan is bought for each day.
