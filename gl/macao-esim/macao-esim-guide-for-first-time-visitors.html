A first trip to Macao, with the phone ready

A first trip to Macao is easier with one less thing to figure out on arrival.

Published March 5, 2026 · Updated August 4, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Sharing data with laptops or family devices

Mobile data disappears through ordinary actions: map refreshes, ride confirmations, translation, ticket screens, restaurant searches, and messages to the people waiting for you. For maps, messages, ride apps, and hotspot use, keep large updates, backups, and streaming on Wi-Fi. If a laptop hotspot or family sharing is part of the trip, choose more daily headroom than a maps-only traveler would need.

Sharing a connection changes the arithmetic. Every device connected through your phone draws on the same allowance, including operating system updates and sync you did not ask for. Whether sharing is allowed at all depends on the plan's hotspot terms, which are listed on the plan page.

Size the data from what you do, not from how long you stay. Maps, messaging and browsing use little. Video calls, uploads and hotspot sharing use a lot, and they tend to fall on the same days. Start from the busiest day you can picture and check that the plan covers it.

The Macao plans

As listed when these notes were last updated. The plan page keeps the current prices and terms, and any plan can be topped up or extended while it runs.

The list for Macao has 22 plans: 8 priced per day and 14 with a fixed allowance. Daily plans run from 1.99 to 13.79 USD, with allowances from 500MB to 10GB per day. Fixed plans run from 3.55 to 66.99 USD, with validity of 7, 15, 30 or 180 days.

Among the fixed plans, Macao eSIM 20GB 30 Days is the cheapest per gigabyte, at about 1.03 USD per GB. The longest, China (mainland HK Macao) eSIM 50GB 180 Days, works out to about 0.37 USD per day over 180 days.

Validity comes in steps, and trips do not. A trip of 8 days is one day longer than the 7-day plans, so the next fit is the 15-day plans. Counting arrival and departure days decides which step you need.

On the daily side, ten days at the smallest allowance (500MB per day) comes to 19.90 USD, and ten days at the largest (10GB per day) to 137.90 USD, if a daily plan is bought for each day.

A few questions

When should I install it?

At home, on good Wi-Fi, a day or so before leaving.

What is the least a Macao plan costs here?

1.99 USD, for Macao eSIM 500MB/Day.

How does the eSIM arrive?

By email, with a QR code and instructions. Keep the email until you are home.

How many plans are there to choose from?

22. 8 reset daily and 14 hold a fixed amount.

Can the home SIM stay in the phone?

Yes. Keep it for calls and messages and use the eSIM for data.

Does the phone need anything special?

It needs eSIM support and must be unlocked by your home carrier.

Where are the current price and hotspot terms?

On the plan page, which always carries the current price, coverage and hotspot terms.
