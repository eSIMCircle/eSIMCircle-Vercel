Traveling Paraguay without roaming charges

Two settings and a prepaid eSIM are usually enough to keep roaming charges away on a Paraguay trip.

Published September 18, 2025 · Updated September 11, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Choosing between daily flexibility and fixed data

Mobile data disappears through ordinary actions: map refreshes, ride confirmations, translation, ticket screens, restaurant searches, and messages to the people waiting for you. For comparing data plans before checkout, keep large updates, backups, and streaming on Wi-Fi. If a laptop hotspot or family sharing is part of the trip, choose more daily headroom than a maps-only traveler would need.

A useful way to estimate is to list the apps you will rely on and ask which of them you would miss most. Navigation and ride apps run for hours on travel days, while messaging barely registers.

Sharing a connection changes the arithmetic. Every device connected through your phone draws on the same allowance, including operating system updates and sync you did not ask for. Whether sharing is allowed at all depends on the plan's hotspot terms, which are listed on the plan page.

Where savings, flexibility, and convenience meet

This section keeps the focus on comparing data plans before checkout for Paraguay. Before choosing, picture the exact moments when being offline would be annoying: arrival, transport, check-in, route changes, or a payment screen. The right eSIM is the one that covers those moments without making you overbuy for days when hotel Wi-Fi will do most of the work.

The first hour after landing is when a missing connection costs the most: a map, a message to whoever is meeting you, a ride, and the address of your first stop. If the eSIM is installed beforehand, all four are available the moment you switch it on.

An eSIM is not a substitute for preparation. Save the address of your first stop, your tickets and an offline map before you leave, so a gap in signal costs minutes and not the afternoon.

The list for Paraguay has 10 plans: 3 priced per day and 7 with a fixed allowance. Daily plans run from 4.39 to 11.29 USD, with allowances from 500MB to 2GB per day. Fixed plans run from 2.99 to 53.55 USD, with validity of 7, 15 or 30 days.

Paraguay eSIM 20GB 30 Days is both the longest fixed plan and the cheapest per gigabyte. It works out to about 2.68 USD per GB and 1.78 USD per day over 30 days.

Validity comes in steps, and trips do not. A trip of 8 days is one day longer than the 7-day plans, so the next fit is the 15-day plans. Counting arrival and departure days decides which step you need.

On the daily side, ten days at the smallest allowance (500MB per day) comes to 43.90 USD, and ten days at the largest (2GB per day) to 112.90 USD, if a daily plan is bought for each day.

A few questions

Is the least expensive Paraguay plan enough?

Often, for a short trip with light use. For longer trips, a plan with a little room left over tends to be the calmer choice.

Yes. Many travelers keep it for calls and SMS and use the eSIM for data.
