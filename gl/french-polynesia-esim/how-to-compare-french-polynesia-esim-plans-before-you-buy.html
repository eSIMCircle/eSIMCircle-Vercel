How to estimate usage without overbuying

Mobile data disappears through ordinary actions: map refreshes, ride confirmations, translation, ticket screens, restaurant searches, and messages to the people waiting for you. For tourist data use before and after arrival, keep large updates, backups, and streaming on Wi-Fi. If a laptop hotspot or family sharing is part of the trip, choose more daily headroom than a maps-only traveler would need.

Plan selection notes for this article scenario

Read the plan cards as a fit check, not a scoreboard. Fixed-data plans are useful when your schedule and data habits are predictable. Daily or flexible plans are better when some days may be heavier than others. Compare French Polynesia eSIM options by validity, data ceiling, hotspot expectations, and setup timing before price becomes the deciding factor.

  • Use daily plans for uncertain usage patterns.
  • Check whether your phone supports eSIM before comparing prices.
  • Use fixed-data plans when your usage is predictable.
  • Compare total data, validity, and route complexity together.

The comparison criteria that matter most

This section keeps the focus on tourist data use before and after arrival for French Polynesia. Before choosing, picture the exact moments when being offline would be annoying: arrival, transport, check-in, route changes, or a payment screen. The right eSIM is the one that covers those moments without making you overbuy for days when hotel Wi-Fi will do most of the work.
