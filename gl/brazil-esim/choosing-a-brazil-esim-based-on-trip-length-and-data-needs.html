A Brazil eSIM sized to the length of the trip

Start with the calendar. The number of days in Brazil, including the two travel days, sets the plan.

Published February 11, 2026 · Updated August 12, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Usage patterns that change the best choice

Mobile data disappears through ordinary actions: map refreshes, ride confirmations, translation, ticket screens, restaurant searches, and messages to the people waiting for you. For price checks and roaming savings, keep large updates, backups, and streaming on Wi-Fi. If a laptop hotspot or family sharing is part of the trip, choose more daily headroom than a maps-only traveler would need.

If you cannot decide between two sizes, take the smaller one and keep the option to top up. Every plan can be topped up with more data or extended with more days while it is active, so a first guess is allowed to be wrong.

If you will share your connection on most days, a larger or daily allowance fits better than a small fixed one. If sharing is an occasional need, a smaller plan with room to top up is usually enough.

Why the cheapest option is not always the safest

This section keeps the focus on price checks and roaming savings for Brazil. Before choosing, picture the exact moments when being offline would be annoying: arrival, transport, check-in, route changes, or a payment screen. The right eSIM is the one that covers those moments without making you overbuy for days when hotel Wi-Fi will do most of the work.

A plan that ends a day early, or one that empties on the busiest day, often costs more in the end than one sized correctly from the start, because you buy a second plan to cover the gap.

The simplest check is to write the trip as a list of dates and put the plan's validity beside it. If the plan falls short at either end, choose the next step up before you pay, not after the plan has expired.

  • Match validity to the full trip, including arrival and departure days.
  • Choose extra data if you expect hotspot use.
  • Use Wi-Fi for large app updates and video backups.
  • Check whether top-up is available for the selected plan.

The Brazil plans

As listed when these notes were last updated. The plan page keeps the current prices and terms, and any plan can be topped up or extended while it runs.

The list for Brazil has 14 plans: 5 priced per day and 9 with a fixed allowance. Daily plans run from 2.59 to 17.89 USD, with allowances from 500MB to 10GB per day. Fixed plans run from 2.55 to 104.55 USD, with validity of 7, 15 or 30 days.

Brazil eSIM 50GB 30 Days is both the longest fixed plan and the cheapest per gigabyte. It works out to about 2.09 USD per GB and 3.48 USD per day over 30 days.

Validity comes in steps, and trips do not. A trip of 8 days is one day longer than the 7-day plans, so the next fit is the 15-day plans. Counting arrival and departure days decides which step you need.

On the daily side, ten days at the smallest allowance (500MB per day) comes to 25.90 USD, and ten days at the largest (10GB per day) to 178.90 USD, if a daily plan is bought for each day.
