When unlimited data is worth it in Maldives

Unlimited-style data is a comfort on some Maldives trips and an expense on others.

Published January 19, 2026 · Updated September 1, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Which travel style matches which plan type

This section keeps the focus on comparing data plans before checkout for Maldives. Before choosing, picture the exact moments when being offline would be annoying: arrival, transport, check-in, route changes, or a payment screen. The right eSIM is the one that covers those moments without making you overbuy for days when hotel Wi-Fi will do most of the work.

The first hour after landing is when a missing connection costs the most: a map, a message to whoever is meeting you, a ride, and the address of your first stop. If the eSIM is installed beforehand, all four are available the moment you switch it on.

An eSIM is not a substitute for preparation. Save the address of your first stop, your tickets and an offline map before you leave, so a gap in signal costs minutes and not the afternoon.

Data habits that change the right plan choice

Mobile data disappears through ordinary actions: map refreshes, ride confirmations, translation, ticket screens, restaurant searches, and messages to the people waiting for you. For comparing data plans before checkout, keep large updates, backups, and streaming on Wi-Fi. If a laptop hotspot or family sharing is part of the trip, choose more daily headroom than a maps-only traveler would need.

Keep large app updates, cloud backups and long uploads for Wi-Fi. They are the quiet drain on any allowance, because phones start them the moment they see a connection.

A longer stay adds uncertainty, and the answer to uncertainty is flexibility. Pick a plan you can extend, size it to the routine you expect rather than the first week, and add days when the trip grows.

The Maldives plans

As listed when these notes were last updated. The plan page keeps the current prices and terms, and any plan can be topped up or extended while it runs.

The list for Maldives has 8 plans: 2 priced per day and 6 with a fixed allowance. Daily plans run from 8.59 to 20.49 USD, with allowances from 1GB to 3GB per day. Fixed plans run from 13.55 to 194.55 USD, with validity of 7, 15 or 30 days.

Maldives eSIM 20GB 30 Days is both the longest fixed plan and the cheapest per gigabyte. It works out to about 9.73 USD per GB and 6.49 USD per day over 30 days.

Validity comes in steps, and trips do not. A trip of 8 days is one day longer than the 7-day plans, so the next fit is the 15-day plans. Counting arrival and departure days decides which step you need.

On the daily side, ten days at the smallest allowance (1GB per day) comes to 85.90 USD, and ten days at the largest (3GB per day) to 204.90 USD, if a daily plan is bought for each day.

A few questions

Who are daily plans for?

Travelers whose phones work hard every day: a laptop on hotspot, video calls, regular uploads.

What is the least a Maldives plan costs here?

8.59 USD, for Maldives eSIM 1GB/Day.

May I share the connection?

Each plan has its own hotspot rules, listed on the plan page.

How many plans are there to choose from?

8. 2 reset daily and 6 hold a fixed amount.

Can the home SIM stay in the phone?

Yes. Keep it for calls and messages and use the eSIM for data.

Can I buy for the whole group at once?

Yes. Several eSIMs can be bought in one purchase.

Do arrival and departure days count towards validity?

Yes. Count both in the validity window, especially if transfers stretch across long travel days.
