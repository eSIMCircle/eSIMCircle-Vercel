Choosing between daily flexibility and fixed data

Mobile data disappears through ordinary actions: map refreshes, ride confirmations, translation, ticket screens, restaurant searches, and messages to the people waiting for you. For price checks and roaming savings, keep large updates, backups, and streaming on Wi-Fi. If a laptop hotspot or family sharing is part of the trip, choose more daily headroom than a maps-only traveler would need.

If you cannot decide between two sizes, take the smaller one and keep the option to top up. Every plan can be topped up with more data or extended with more days while it is active, so a first guess is allowed to be wrong.

Sharing a connection changes the arithmetic. Every device connected through your phone draws on the same allowance, including operating system updates and sync you did not ask for. Whether sharing is allowed at all depends on the plan's hotspot terms, which are listed on the plan page.

Where savings, flexibility, and convenience meet

This section keeps the focus on price checks and roaming savings for Costa Rica. Before choosing, picture the exact moments when being offline would be annoying: arrival, transport, check-in, route changes, or a payment screen. The right eSIM is the one that covers those moments without making you overbuy for days when hotel Wi-Fi will do most of the work.

When a phone shows no mobile data after landing, check the settings before blaming the network. Confirm the eSIM is selected as the line for mobile data and that data roaming is on for that line. Then give it a few minutes, restart the phone, and check the selection again.

Two things make installation go smoothly: a stable Wi-Fi connection and the QR email saved somewhere you can open without mobile data. Everything else, including switching the eSIM on, can wait until you land.

The Costa Rica plans

As listed when these notes were last updated. The plan page keeps the current prices and terms, and any plan can be topped up or extended while it runs.

The list for Costa Rica has 9 plans: 3 priced per day and 6 with a fixed allowance. Daily plans run from 2.29 to 6.49 USD, with allowances from 500MB to 2GB per day. Fixed plans run from 5.55 to 61.55 USD, with validity of 7, 15 or 30 days.

Costa Rica eSIM 20GB 30 Days is both the longest fixed plan and the cheapest per gigabyte. It works out to about 3.08 USD per GB and 2.05 USD per day over 30 days.

Validity comes in steps, and trips do not. A trip of 8 days is one day longer than the 7-day plans, so the next fit is the 15-day plans. Counting arrival and departure days decides which step you need.

On the daily side, ten days at the smallest allowance (500MB per day) comes to 22.90 USD, and ten days at the largest (2GB per day) to 64.90 USD, if a daily plan is bought for each day.

A few questions

When should I install it?

At home, on good Wi-Fi, a day or so before leaving.

What is the least a Costa Rica plan costs here?

2.29 USD, for Costa Rica eSIM 500MB/Day.

How does the eSIM arrive?

By email, with a QR code and instructions. Keep the email until you are home.

How many plans are there to choose from?

9. 3 reset daily and 6 hold a fixed amount.

Can the home SIM stay in the phone?

Yes. Keep it for calls and messages and use the eSIM for data.

Do arrival and departure days count towards validity?

Yes. Count both in the validity window, especially if transfers stretch across long travel days.

Does the phone need anything special?

It needs eSIM support and must be unlocked by your home carrier.
