The price of travel data in Kenya, for short and long trips

Price makes sense only next to the days and data a plan gives. Here is how they line up for Kenya.

Published October 6, 2026 · Updated October 19, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

How to estimate usage without overbuying

Mobile data disappears through ordinary actions: map refreshes, ride confirmations, translation, ticket screens, restaurant searches, and messages to the people waiting for you. For activation, coverage, and data questions, keep large updates, backups, and streaming on Wi-Fi. If a laptop hotspot or family sharing is part of the trip, choose more daily headroom than a maps-only traveler would need.

If you cannot decide between two sizes, take the smaller one and keep the option to top up. Every plan can be topped up with more data or extended with more days while it is active, so a first guess is allowed to be wrong.

If you will share your connection on most days, a larger or daily allowance fits better than a small fixed one. If sharing is an occasional need, a smaller plan with room to top up is usually enough.

Plan selection notes for this article scenario

Read the plan cards as a fit check, not a scoreboard. Fixed-data plans are useful when your schedule and data habits are predictable. Daily or flexible plans are better when some days may be heavier than others. Compare Kenya eSIM options by validity, data ceiling, hotspot expectations, and setup timing before price becomes the deciding factor.

The simplest check is to write the trip as a list of dates and put the plan's validity beside it. If the plan falls short at either end, choose the next step up before you pay, not after the plan has expired.

Price is one number on a list that also has days and data. Read all three before you choose. A plan that costs a little more but covers every day and your busiest day is better value than a cheaper one that leaves a gap.

  • Check that the device is carrier-unlocked and eSIM compatible.
  • Set the eSIM as the mobile data line, not necessarily the voice line.
  • Install on Wi-Fi before the trip when possible.
  • Keep the QR email until the trip is over.

A practical setup timeline for Kenya

Set up the eSIM while you still have steady Wi-Fi and time to read the prompts. Save the QR email, name the travel line clearly, select it for mobile data, and turn on roaming for that line if the instructions require it. If service does not appear immediately, restart the phone and wait for network registration before deleting anything.

Plan the installation for a day or two before you fly. That is early enough to sort out a problem, and late enough that the QR email is still easy to find.

Keep large app updates, cloud backups and long uploads for Wi-Fi. They are the quiet drain on any allowance, because phones start them the moment they see a connection.

The Kenya plans

As listed when these notes were last updated. The plan page keeps the current prices and terms, and any plan can be topped up or extended while it runs.

The list for Kenya has 13 plans: 5 priced per day and 8 with a fixed allowance. Daily plans run from 2.69 to 19.39 USD, with allowances from 500MB to 10GB per day. Fixed plans run from 2.99 to 113.55 USD, with validity of 7, 15 or 30 days.

Kenya eSIM 20GB 30 Days is both the longest fixed plan and the cheapest per gigabyte. It works out to about 5.68 USD per GB and 3.78 USD per day over 30 days.

Validity comes in steps, and trips do not. A trip of 8 days is one day longer than the 7-day plans, so the next fit is the 15-day plans. Counting arrival and departure days decides which step you need.

On the daily side, ten days at the smallest allowance (500MB per day) comes to 26.90 USD, and ten days at the largest (10GB per day) to 193.90 USD, if a daily plan is bought for each day.

A few questions

Is the least expensive Kenya plan enough?

Often, for a short trip with light use. For longer trips, a plan with a little room left over tends to be the calmer choice.

What is the least a Kenya plan costs here?

2.69 USD, for Kenya eSIM 500MB/Day.

Can more data be added along the way?

Yes. Any plan can be topped up or extended along the way, as long as it is still active.

Can the home SIM stay in the phone?

Yes. Keep it for calls and messages and use the eSIM for data.
