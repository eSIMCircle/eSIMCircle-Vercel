What to download before weak-signal moments

This section keeps the focus on tourist data use before and after arrival for Latvia. Before choosing, picture the exact moments when being offline would be annoying: arrival, transport, check-in, route changes, or a payment screen. The right eSIM is the one that covers those moments without making you overbuy for days when hotel Wi-Fi will do most of the work.

Plan selection notes for this article scenario

Read the plan cards as a fit check, not a scoreboard. Fixed-data plans are useful when your schedule and data habits are predictable. Daily or flexible plans are better when some days may be heavier than others. Compare Latvia eSIM options by validity, data ceiling, hotspot expectations, and setup timing before price becomes the deciding factor.

  • Avoid assuming indoor basement coverage will match street-level signal.
  • Keep messaging apps ready for lower-signal moments.
  • Download maps before remote routes.
  • Restart the phone after long border or ferry transfers.

Where should a comparison begin?

With the number of days the plan lasts. Then the data, then the price.

Does my phone need to be unlocked?

Yes, and it needs to support eSIM.

Can the home SIM stay in the phone?

Yes. Keep it for calls and messages and use the eSIM for data.
