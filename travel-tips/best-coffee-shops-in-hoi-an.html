Hoi An

The best coffee shops in Hoi An

Vietnamese coffee is strong, sweet and slow to drip, which suits Hoi An perfectly. The best cafés here are usually a street or two away from the busiest lanes.

Published June 1, 2026 · Updated August 4, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

The old town edges

The pedestrian heart of the old town is crowded and pricier. Just outside it, smaller cafés have easier seating and views of the old houses without the crush.

An Bang

By the beach, An Bang's lanes have casual coffee, smoothies and light sweets that fit a slow afternoon.

Cam Chau

Between the old town and the beach, Cam Chau is calmer, with more space and a slower pace.

Tra Que side roads

Near the vegetable village, small garden cafés offer shade and distance from the crowds, especially pleasant in the heat of the day.

What to order

OrderWhat it is
Cà phê sữa đáIced coffee with sweetened condensed milk
Cà phê đenBlack coffee, often iced
Cà phê muốiSalt coffee, with a salty cream topping
Coconut coffeeCoffee blended with coconut milk or ice cream

Vietnamese coffee is very strong. If caffeine keeps you awake, have it in the morning.

When to go

Early morning is excellent, before the old town heats up. Mid-afternoon suits shaded garden cafés or An Bang. The centre gets busiest towards evening.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was roughly ₫30,000 to ₫70,000 and more elaborate desserts or café sweets ₫40,000 to ₫120,000.

Getting around

Hoi An addresses can sit down narrow lanes. Save the pin before walking or cycling. Parts of the old town are pedestrian-only at certain times, so a taxi may not reach every address. Cycling to Tra Que or An Bang is pleasant early, but avoid long rides in the midday heat just for one café.

A few questions

What is cà phê sữa đá?

Vietnamese iced coffee with sweetened condensed milk.

Where are the quietest cafés in Hoi An?

Just outside the old town's pedestrian core, in Cam Chau, and in garden cafés near Tra Que.

Can taxis reach cafés in Hoi An's old town?

Not always. Parts of the old town are pedestrian-only at certain times.
