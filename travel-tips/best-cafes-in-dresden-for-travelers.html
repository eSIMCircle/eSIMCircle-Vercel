The best cafés in Dresden for travelers

Dresden has two café personalities, divided by the Elbe. The Altstadt keeps the tradition of coffee and cake; the Neustadt across the river has the independent coffee scene.

Published December 22, 2025 · Updated September 5, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

The Neustadt

North of the river, the Neustadt is the strongest area for specialty coffee, creative bakeries and relaxed seating. Its side streets have small spaces with neighbourhood energy, easy to linger in.

The Altstadt

Close to the Frauenkirche and the main sights, traditional cafés serve coffee and cake in grand surroundings. Convenient between sightseeing stops.

The Äußere Neustadt side streets

Beyond the main streets of the Outer Neustadt, smaller cafés make it easy to sit for a while. Note that the Inner Neustadt and the Outer Neustadt are different areas; check which one a café is in.

Striesen

East of the centre, residential Striesen is a quiet option away from the crowds.

What to order

Dresdner Eierschecke, a layered cake of pastry, quark and custard, is the city's own. Order it with coffee in the afternoon, but not too late: popular slices sell out. In winter, Stollen, the rich fruit bread, is everywhere.

The German tradition of Kaffee und Kuchen, coffee and cake in the afternoon, is alive in Dresden. It is a good excuse to slow down.

When to go

Weekday mornings are calm in the Neustadt. Traditional cake cafés are busiest on weekend afternoons.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was about €2.50 to €4.50 and cake or pastry €3.50 to €7 in most cafés.

Crossing the river

The Altstadt and the Neustadt look close on a map, but the walk across the bridge and through the districts takes time. Trams make the trip easy. Not every bakery counter is happy with a long laptop stay; save that for a café with seating.

A few questions

Where is the best area for cafés in Dresden?

The Neustadt for specialty coffee; the Altstadt for traditional coffee and cake.

When should I eat Eierschecke?

In the afternoon with coffee, but not too late, as popular slices sell out.

Is the Inner Neustadt the same as the Outer Neustadt?

No. They are different areas, so check which one a café is in.
