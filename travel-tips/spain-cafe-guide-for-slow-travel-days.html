A Spain café guide for slow travel days, from Valencia

Spanish café life runs through the whole day: coffee in the morning, a drink in the afternoon, something sweet in the evening. In Valencia, the afternoon belongs to horchata.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Ciutat Vella

The old town has cafés, horchaterías and pastry shops within easy walking distance of the main sights.

Ruzafa

A lively neighbourhood with independent coffee shops, bakeries and modern dessert cafés. Busy at brunch and on weekend afternoons.

Colón and the Eixample

Around Calle Colón, café terraces and pastry stops sit among the shopping streets.

Alboraya

Just north of the city, Alboraya is known for its tiger nuts and horchata. A trip there is a small outing in itself.

What to order

OrderWhat it is
Café con lecheCoffee with milk, the morning standard
CortadoEspresso with a little milk
HorchataA cold, sweet drink made from tiger nuts
FartonsLong, soft, sugar-glazed buns for dipping in horchata

Taste the horchata before ordering several fartons. Both are sweet, and two fartons per person is usually plenty.

When to go

Morning coffee is relaxed. Horchata makes a natural afternoon break. Ruzafa gets busier towards brunch and on weekend afternoons.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was roughly €1.50 to €3.50, horchata €2.50 to €5, and pastries or simple desserts €2 to €6.

Practical notes

Not every café serves fresh horchata all year, and some horchaterías close for part of the year. Do not leave it until your last evening. Valencia is easy on foot, by metro and by bus; treat a trip to Alboraya as its own outing rather than squeezing it between city-centre stops.

A few questions

What is horchata?

A cold, sweet drink made from tiger nuts, a Valencian speciality.

Where does horchata come from?

Alboraya, just north of Valencia, is famous for its tiger nuts and horchata.

Is horchata available all year?

Not everywhere. Some horchaterías close for part of the year, so check before you go.
