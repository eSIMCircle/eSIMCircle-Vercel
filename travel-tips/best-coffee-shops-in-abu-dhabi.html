The best coffee shops in Abu Dhabi

Abu Dhabi is spread out and hot, so coffee here is about choosing a district and settling in, not hopping between cafés on foot.

Published June 10, 2026 · Updated August 2, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Al Bateen

A comfortable residential and waterfront district with polished cafés, good for long sit-down breaks.

Mamsha Al Saadiyat

On Saadiyat Island near the Louvre Abu Dhabi, Mamsha's beachfront promenade has modern coffee spaces and desserts. A natural pause after a museum visit.

Yas Island

Hotel and mall cafés on Yas Island make it easy to find reliable coffee and baked goods between attractions.

Al Zeina and Al Raha Beach

Quieter waterfront areas with spacious seating, good for families or anyone avoiding crowds.

What to order

Alongside specialty coffee, try two local traditions. Arabic coffee, gahwa, is lightly roasted, often spiced with cardamom and served in small cups, usually with dates. Karak is strong, sweet, spiced milky tea, loved across the Gulf and sold everywhere from roadside stalls to smart cafés.

Arabic coffee is traditionally offered as a gesture of hospitality. If you are served it, a small cup or two is the polite amount; gently shake the cup when you have had enough.

When to go

Morning and late afternoon are easiest for moving around. Outdoor terraces are comfortable outside the hottest part of the day, and in the cooler months.

What it costs

Before publication, specialty coffee was about AED18 to AED30 and plated desserts or premium cakes AED25 to AED45. Karak from a simple stall was far cheaper.

Getting around

Abu Dhabi is not built for long walks between cafés. Group your stops by district and use taxis or ride-hailing between them. Hotel and mall cafés may have their own parking or access rules. Not every waterfront café has shaded outdoor seating.

A few questions

What is karak tea?

Strong, sweet, spiced milky tea, popular across the Gulf.

What is Arabic coffee like?

Lightly roasted and often spiced with cardamom, served in small cups with dates.

Can I walk between cafés in Abu Dhabi?

Within a district, sometimes. Between districts, use a taxi or ride-hailing.
