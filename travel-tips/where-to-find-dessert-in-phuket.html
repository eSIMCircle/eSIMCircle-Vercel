Where to find dessert in Phuket

Phuket's sweet side mixes Thai desserts with the Peranakan traditions of its Old Town, plus beach cafés with sunset views. It is an island, not a city, so plan by area.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Phuket Old Town

The colourful Sino-Portuguese shophouses of the Old Town hold cafés and dessert shops, including places selling Peranakan sweets. Easiest in the morning, before the heat and foot traffic build.

The west-coast beaches

Kata, Karon and Patong have cafés and dessert spots for beach days. Calmer before lunch.

Rawai and the south

Near the island's southern tip, sunset-view cafés are busiest in the late afternoon.

What to order

DessertWhat it is
Mango sticky riceSweet sticky rice with fresh mango and coconut milk
KhanomA family of Thai sweets made with rice flour, coconut and palm sugar
Peranakan sweetsColourful cakes in Phuket Old Town, linked to the island's Chinese heritage
Thai teaStrong, spiced, orange-coloured tea with condensed milk, often iced
Coconut ice creamOften served in a coconut shell, with toppings

Mango sticky rice is best in mango season, roughly from spring into early summer.

When to go

The Old Town is easiest in the morning. Beach cafés are calmer before lunch, and sunset spots are busiest in the late afternoon.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was roughly ฿60 to ฿120 and desserts ฿80 to ฿180 in tourist-oriented cafés. Premium beach venues cost more.

Getting around

Phuket is not one walkable café district. The Old Town and the west-coast beaches are far apart. Use ride-hailing between areas, and cluster stops by area. In the rainy season, plan for showers, and check hours: some cafés change them in low season. Cafés inside hotels or gated complexes may have limited access.

A few questions

Where are the best dessert cafés in Phuket?

Phuket Old Town has many, and the west-coast beaches have more.

When is mango sticky rice best?

In mango season, roughly from spring into early summer.

Can I walk from Phuket Old Town to the beaches?

No. They are far apart; use ride-hailing.
