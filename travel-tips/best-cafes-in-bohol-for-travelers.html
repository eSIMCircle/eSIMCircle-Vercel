The best cafés in Bohol for travelers

Bohol's cafés are scattered across an island, not packed into a city. The best approach is to enjoy the ones near where you are staying and treat the rest as part of a day out.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Panglao and the Alona area

Around Alona Beach, Panglao has the widest choice of cafés, smoothie bars and dessert spots for visitors staying near the beaches. Expect busy brunches and sunset crowds.

Dauis

Between Panglao and Tagbilaran, Dauis makes a good slower stop, perhaps combined with a visit to its old church.

Tagbilaran

Bohol's main city has everyday coffee shops and bakeries, often cheaper than the resort areas, and a more local feel.

Anda

On the far east coast, Anda has a quieter beach scene and its own cafés. It is far enough away to be a separate trip, best for travelers already staying there.

What to order

Fresh fruit shakes, especially mango, are the island staple. Many cafés serve Filipino coffee, sometimes from local beans, alongside espresso drinks. For something sweet, look for ube desserts and Bohol's calamay, a sticky coconut and rice sweet.

A café visit in Bohol is a good excuse to try ube, the purple yam that turns up in cakes, ice cream and drinks.

When to go

Morning coffee is easiest before the island heat builds. Panglao's cafés get busier at brunch and again around sunset.

What it costs

Before publication, around Panglao a coffee was roughly ₱100 to ₱180 and a dessert or bigger café plate ₱150 to ₱300. Tagbilaran was often cheaper.

Getting between cafés

Bohol is not one walkable café zone. Panglao and Tagbilaran need a tricycle, a car or ride-hailing between them, and Anda is a day's journey in itself. Do not make a long cross-island trip for one café.

Mobile signal can be weaker in rural parts of the island, so save addresses offline. At beach cafés, check for shade before sitting down in the midday sun.

A few questions

Where are most cafés in Bohol?

Around Panglao and Alona Beach, with more everyday options in Tagbilaran.

Is Anda close to Panglao?

No. Anda is on the far east coast and is best treated as a separate trip.

What is ube?

A purple yam used in Filipino desserts, cakes and drinks.
