Where to find dessert in Gangnam

Gangnam's dessert cafés are polished, inventive and spread across several neighbourhoods. Choose one cluster per afternoon; trying to cover them all in one go means more time on the subway than at the table.

Published November 11, 2025 · Updated September 21, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Garosu-gil, Sinsa

The tree-lined street near Sinsa Station and its side streets have boutique cafés and dessert shops. The easiest place to start.

Apgujeong Rodeo

Fashion streets near Apgujeong Rodeo Station have dessert cafés alongside designer shops.

Cheongdam

Gangnam's most upscale area, with premium cafés and patisseries, and prices to match.

Around COEX

The COEX complex and the streets near Samseong Station have mall cafés and bakeries, useful in bad weather.

What to order

DessertWhat it is
BingsuShaved milk ice piled with fruit, red bean or other toppings
CroffleA croissant pressed in a waffle iron, often with toppings
HotteokA sweet filled pancake, sold at street stalls
Seasonal cakesStrawberry cakes in winter and spring are a Korean café favourite

Korean dessert trends move fast. A café that went viral last year may have moved or closed, so check recent listings.

When to go

Weekday mornings are calm. Dessert cafés around Garosu-gil and Apgujeong get busier from mid-afternoon.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was roughly ₩5,000 to ₩8,000 and dessert ₩7,000 to ₩15,000. Premium Cheongdam cafés can cost more.

Getting around

Gangnam is not one walkable neighbourhood. Garosu-gil is easiest from Sinsa Station, while Apgujeong Rodeo and Cheongdam are separate clusters. Use the subway between them, and save the exit for each café in Naver Map or KakaoMap.

A few questions

What is bingsu?

A Korean shaved milk ice dessert with fruit, red bean or other toppings.

Where is Garosu-gil?

In Sinsa, Gangnam, near Sinsa Station.

Can I walk between Garosu-gil and Cheongdam?

They are separate clusters. The subway is easier.
