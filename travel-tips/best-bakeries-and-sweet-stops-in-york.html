The best bakeries and sweet stops in York

York does tea rooms well. Its medieval streets are full of places for a pot of tea and a slice of cake, and a few independent coffee shops for when you want something stronger.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Stonegate and Goodramgate

Close to the Minster, these historic streets have tea rooms, bakeries and small cafés in old buildings. The most atmospheric choice, and the busiest.

Fossgate and Walmgate

A good street for specialty coffee and a less formal break, with independent cafés and bakeries.

Micklegate

On the way in from the railway station, Micklegate makes a calm first stop when you arrive.

Bishopthorpe Road

South of the centre, "Bishy Road" is a local high street with independent cafés and sweet treats, a short detour from the crowds.

Tea room or café?

If you wantChoose
A quick coffee and cakeAn independent café on Fossgate or Bishopthorpe Road
A traditional pot of teaA tea room near the Minster
A full afternoon teaA booked tea room, with sandwiches, scones and cakes

A full afternoon tea is a meal, not a snack. If you just want a quick break, order tea and a scone instead.

What to try

Scones with jam and clotted cream are a British tea-room classic. Yorkshire specialities include parkin, a sticky ginger and oat cake, and fat rascals, a large rich scone with fruit and peel.

When to go

Arrive before 11am for calmer tea rooms. Weekend afternoons are busiest, especially in the historic centre, and the smallest tea rooms can have queues.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was roughly £3 to £5 and cake £4 to £7, with full afternoon tea costing more.

Practical notes

York's centre is compact but cobbled and crowded. Wear comfortable shoes, and save the exact street, as small cafés can be tucked behind narrow shopfronts. Not every bakery has seating.

A few questions

What is a fat rascal?

A large, rich scone with fruit and peel, associated with Yorkshire.

Do I need to book afternoon tea in York?

For a full afternoon tea at popular tea rooms, booking is a good idea.

When are York's tea rooms quietest?

Before 11am on weekdays.
