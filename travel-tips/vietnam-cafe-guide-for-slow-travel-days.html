Ho Chi Minh City

A Vietnam café guide for slow travel days, from Ho Chi Minh City

Ho Chi Minh City has one of the world's great café cultures. Some of its best cafés hide upstairs in old apartment blocks or at the end of narrow alleys, which is part of the fun.

Published March 16, 2026 · Updated August 3, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

District 1

Historic buildings, upper-floor cafés and modern specialty shops sit close to the main sights. Some old apartment blocks have several cafés on different floors. Busiest from late morning.

District 3

A calmer mix of old villas, contemporary cafés and strong Vietnamese coffee. Excellent before lunch.

Thảo Điền

Across the river, Thảo Điền has spacious, design-led cafés and bakeries. Worth half a day on its own.

Phú Nhuận

North of the centre, neighbourhood coffee and desserts outside the tourist core.

What to order

OrderWhat it is
Cà phê sữa đáIced coffee with condensed milk, the city's classic
Bạc xỉuMore milk than coffee, sweeter and milder
Cà phê trứngEgg coffee, a creamy whipped egg topping, originally from Hanoi
Cà phê muốiSalt coffee, with a salty cream topping

Condensed-milk drinks are very sweet. One is plenty before you try another café.

When to go

Coffee starts early here. District 3 is excellent before lunch; District 1 gets busy from late morning; Thảo Điền suits a long afternoon.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was roughly ₫35,000 to ₫80,000 and dessert ₫40,000 to ₫120,000 in modern cafés. Neighbourhood stops are often cheaper.

Finding the entrance

Many cafés are upstairs, inside old apartment blocks or down narrow alleys. Save the building name, the floor and the pin, not only the street number; it is easy to stand at the right address and miss the stairs. Avoid crossing town at rush hour for one café, and remember that not every café is set up for laptop work.

A few questions

What is bạc xỉu?

A milky Vietnamese coffee with more milk than coffee, sweeter and milder than cà phê sữa đá.

Why are cafés hard to find in Ho Chi Minh City?

Many are upstairs in old apartment blocks or down alleys. Save the building name and floor.

Where is Thảo Điền?

Across the river from the centre, known for spacious, design-led cafés.
