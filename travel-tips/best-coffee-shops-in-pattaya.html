Pattaya

The best coffee shops in Pattaya

Pattaya is better known for its nightlife, but its café scene has grown up, with garden cafés, hillside terraces and serious coffee along the coast.

Published September 8, 2025 · Updated August 19, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Jomtien

South of the centre, Jomtien has a mix of specialty coffee, bakeries and larger cafés that suit a slow beach day.

Naklua and Wongamat

North Pattaya's coffee and garden cafés feel less hectic than the centre. Good in the morning.

Pratumnak

The hill between Pattaya and Jomtien has hillside and coastal cafés that are lovely in the late afternoon, with sea views.

Central Pattaya

Easy coffee and dessert stops when you do not want to take a taxi anywhere special.

What to order

Thai iced coffee, oliang or cha yen for the tea version, is strong, sweet and very cold, usually with condensed milk. Modern cafés add Thai flavours such as coconut, pandan or Thai tea to drinks and desserts. Mango sticky rice works as a café dessert, too.

Sunset cafés on Pratumnak fill up quickly. Arrive early and leave time for traffic.

When to go

Naklua is good in the morning. Jomtien and Pratumnak are better later, and sunset-facing cafés can fill fast.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was roughly ฿70 to ฿140 and desserts ฿100 to ฿220 at modern cafés. Beachfront and concept cafés cost more.

Getting around

Pattaya stretches along the coast, and Jomtien, Naklua and the centre are separate café zones. Use baht buses or ride-hailing between them rather than walking the whole strip in the heat. Not every beachfront café is a coffee specialist; some are mainly for views and drinks.

A few questions

What is oliang?

Thai iced coffee, strong and sweet, often with condensed milk.

Where can I find a café with a sea view in Pattaya?

Pratumnak hill has hillside and coastal cafés with sea views.

Can I walk between cafés in Pattaya?

Within an area, yes. Between Jomtien, Naklua and the centre, use transport.
