The best coffee shops in Amsterdam, the ones that serve coffee

A word of warning first. In Amsterdam, a "coffeeshop" usually means a licensed cannabis venue. If you are after espresso and a pastry, you want a café or a koffiebar.

Published July 6, 2026 · Updated August 3, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Searching the right way

Search your map for "café", "koffiebar", "specialty coffee" or "bakery", not "coffeeshop". It will save you an awkward entrance.

The Jordaan

Canal-side streets, bakeries and specialty coffee make the Jordaan a natural slow-morning area. Busy from late breakfast and at weekends.

De Pijp

South of the centre, De Pijp has a dense mix of independent cafés and pastry spots with a neighbourhood feel, near the Albert Cuyp Market.

The Negen Straatjes and the centre

The Nine Streets, between the main canals, are easy for visitors, though seating and prices reflect the location.

Amsterdam Oost

East of the centre, Oost has good specialty coffee and roomier cafés, away from the busiest streets.

What to order

Amsterdam has a serious specialty-coffee scene. Pair a flat white with appeltaart, Dutch apple pie, usually served with whipped cream. A fresh stroopwafel from a market stall is also hard to beat.

Dutch apple pie is deep and generously spiced. One slice is plenty between two.

When to go

Specialty cafés are calmest early. The Jordaan and central cafés get busy around late breakfast and weekend shopping hours.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was roughly €3 to €5.50 and pastry or cake €4 to €8 in most specialty cafés.

Practical notes

Many cafés are card-only. Narrow café tables are in demand at weekend brunch, so avoid staying for hours at peak times. And if you are cycling, be confident in the traffic before you add a strong coffee to the mix.

A few questions

What is a coffeeshop in Amsterdam?

Usually a licensed cannabis venue. For coffee, look for a café or koffiebar.

Which Amsterdam neighbourhood is best for cafés?

The Jordaan and De Pijp are both good, and Oost is quieter.

Do Amsterdam cafés accept cash?

Many are card-only, so carry a card.
