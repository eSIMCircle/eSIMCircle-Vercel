The best bakeries and sweet stops in Yogyakarta

Yogyakarta has a big café culture, from traditional coffee stalls to spacious modern cafés. Its local sweets, especially bakpia, deserve a stop of their own.

Published February 9, 2026 · Updated April 13, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Prawirotaman

A relaxed, creative neighbourhood south of the palace, with many cafés and guesthouses and easy places to sit for a long time. It becomes more sociable later in the day.

Kotabaru

North-east of the centre, Kotabaru's heritage streets and shade make it a comfortable area for coffee and conversation. It is especially good in the morning.

Gejayan

Further north, Gejayan has a wide range of casual coffee shops and dessert spots used by students, with more local prices.

The Kaliurang road

The road running north towards Mount Merapi has larger cafés with more space. They are often busiest after sunset and worth the trip if you want room to spread out.

What to order

Bakpia, small round pastries filled with mung bean or other fillings, are Yogyakarta's best-known sweet. Shops in the Pathuk area sell them by the box, and they make good gifts. In cafés, try Indonesian coffee, sometimes from Javanese beans, and local sweets such as klepon, rice-flour balls filled with palm sugar.

A warung kopi, a simple coffee stall, is different from a laptop-friendly café. Both are worth trying; just do not expect Wi-Fi and power sockets at the first.

When to go

Kotabaru is lovely in the morning. Prawirotaman livens up later, and the big northern cafés peak after sunset.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was roughly Rp20,000 to Rp45,000 and simple local sweets Rp15,000 to Rp40,000. Concept cafés and premium desserts cost more.

Getting around

Yogyakarta's traffic makes short distances slow. Prawirotaman and Kotabaru are good anchors; use Grab or Gojek for Gejayan or the northern cafés. Carry small cash for simpler stops.

A few questions

What is bakpia?

A small round pastry filled with mung bean or other fillings, Yogyakarta's best-known sweet.

Which area has the most cafés in Yogyakarta?

Prawirotaman is a good base, with many cafés close together.

How do I get to the cafés on the Kaliurang road?

Ride-hailing apps such as Grab or Gojek are the easiest way.
