Where to find dessert in Sydney

Sydney's dessert scene follows its coffee culture: early starts, excellent bakeries and Asian dessert shops in Haymarket. Go early; the best pastries sell out.

Published May 19, 2026 · Updated September 6, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Surry Hills

South of the centre, Surry Hills has some of the city's best bakeries and cafés. Signature pastries can sell out by early afternoon.

Newtown and Enmore

The inner west's high streets have bakeries, gelato shops and dessert spots with a relaxed, bohemian feel.

Haymarket and Chinatown

Near Central Station, Haymarket's dessert shops serve Asian sweets: bubble tea, shaved ice, mochi and more, often open late.

The CBD and the harbour

Cafés with harbour views around Circular Quay, best for one special stop.

Sydney coffee culture starts early. Many of the best cafés are at their best before lunch.

When to go

Start early. Bakeries can run out of signature pastries by early afternoon. Haymarket's dessert shops are a good evening option.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was around A$4.50 to A$7 and plated desserts or premium bakery items A$8 to A$16. Some venues add a weekend or public holiday surcharge.

Getting around

Sydney distances add up. Use trains, light rail or buses between neighbourhoods, then walk within each one. Do not cross the city for a single stop.
