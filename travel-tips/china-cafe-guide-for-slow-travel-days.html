Chengdu

A China café guide for slow travel days, from Chengdu

Chengdu is famous for its relaxed pace and its tea houses, where locals spend whole afternoons. Its modern cafés follow the same spirit, which makes it the right city for a slow day.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Start with tea

Before the coffee, spend an hour in a traditional tea house. In Chengdu's parks, especially People's Park, tea houses serve jasmine and other teas in lidded bowls, with hot water refills, while people play mahjong around you. It is one of the city's essential experiences.

Taikoo Li and the Daci Temple area

A polished cluster of specialty coffee, bakeries and desserts next to an old temple, with easy metro access. Busy after lunch and at weekends.

Yulin

A lived-in neighbourhood where independent cafés and small creative spaces sit among homes and shops.

The Kuanzhai side streets

Beyond the busiest pedestrian lanes of Kuanzhai Alley, quieter cafés and pastry stops are easier to enjoy.

Tongzilin

South of the centre, Tongzilin suits a slower café session away from the main sights.

What to order

Modern Chengdu cafés mix specialty coffee with local flavours: tea-based drinks, osmanthus, and sweets less rich than Western desserts. Many popular concept cafés use mobile ordering through an app or a QR code at the table.

Mornings are best for a focused coffee. Photo-friendly dessert cafés fill up after lunch, especially at weekends.

What it costs

Before publication, many modern cafés charged around ¥20 to ¥40 for coffee and ¥25 to ¥60 for desserts, with concept cafés higher. Tea houses are often cheaper.

Practical notes

Save the Chinese name and address of every café; English names vary between apps. Cafés may be inside a mall or courtyard rather than at street level. Group stops by metro area instead of crossing the city repeatedly.

Pay with Alipay or WeChat Pay, set up before you travel. Many apps visitors use are blocked on local Chinese networks; travel eSIMs that route data outside China can usually reach them.
