The best coffee shops in Venice

In Venice, as in the rest of Italy, coffee is usually drunk standing at the bar, quickly, for a fraction of the price of a table on a famous square. Knowing that changes how you use the city's cafés.

Published September 21, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Standing or sitting?

Prices often depend on where you drink. A coffee at the bar is cheapest. The same coffee at a table, especially on Piazza San Marco with an orchestra playing, can cost many times more. Both are valid choices; just check the prices first.

Cannaregio

In the north of the city, Cannaregio is good for espresso and pastries away from the crowds of San Marco.

Dorsoduro

Small squares and quieter lanes in Dorsoduro make it easier to sit without feeling rushed.

The side streets of San Marco

Stay one or two streets away from the busiest routes and you will find better value and easier seating, even close to the main sights.

Castello

On the eastern side, Castello has simple coffee bars and bakeries in a neighbourhood setting.

How to order

OrderWhat you get
Un caffèAn espresso
MacchiatoEspresso with a little milk foam
CappuccinoUsually a morning drink in Italy
Caffè correttoEspresso with a dash of liqueur

Many Italians drink cappuccino only in the morning. You can order it later, but an espresso is the usual choice after lunch.

At busy bars, you sometimes pay at the till first and then take the receipt to the counter. Watch what locals do.

What to eat with it

A cornetto, the Italian croissant, is the classic breakfast. Look also for Venetian sweets such as fritole around Carnival time.

What it costs

Before publication, a standing espresso was roughly €1.50 to €3 and pastry or dessert €4 to €9. Table service in prime spots raises the bill considerably.

Finding your way

Bridges and dead ends make short distances slow. Pin the café entrance and allow extra time. Small bars may not have a customer toilet.

A few questions

Is coffee cheaper at the bar in Venice?

Yes. Standing at the bar is usually much cheaper than sitting at a table, especially on famous squares.

Can I order a cappuccino in the afternoon in Italy?

You can, but many Italians drink it only in the morning.

What is a cornetto?

The Italian version of a croissant, a classic breakfast with coffee.
