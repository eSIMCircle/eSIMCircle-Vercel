Where to find dessert in Lyon

Lyon's food reputation extends to its pastry. Its signature colour is the bright pink of praline, and its pâtisseries and chocolatiers are among the best in France.

Published June 24, 2026 · Updated June 24, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

The Presqu'île

The peninsula between the rivers has pâtisseries, chocolatiers and tea rooms along its shopping streets. A good place to begin.

Terreaux

Around the Place des Terreaux, near the fine arts museum, cafés and chocolate shops make a natural second stop.

Croix-Rousse

Up the hill, Croix-Rousse has neighbourhood bakeries and a village feel.

Vieux Lyon

The old town has pastry shops among its Renaissance lanes, some aimed squarely at visitors.

What to order

SweetWhat it is
Tarte aux pralinesA tart filled with melted pink pralines, Lyon's signature
Brioche aux pralinesSoft brioche studded with pink pralines
Coussin de LyonA small green, cushion-shaped sweet of chocolate and marzipan
BugnesLight fried pastry, traditionally around Carnival

Praline tart is very sweet and rich. Share a slice, or have it as your one dessert of the day.

When to go

Late morning is good for pâtisserie counters. Tea rooms are busier mid-afternoon, and Saturdays around the central shopping streets are the most crowded. Signature items at small pâtisseries can sell out late in the day.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was about €2.50 to €5 and an individual pastry or dessert €4 to €9. Premium chocolate and elaborate pâtisserie cost more.

A short route

Begin on the Presqu'île with coffee and one pastry. Walk towards Terreaux or Croix-Rousse for chocolate or tea. Save a light sweet for later rather than three rich desserts in a row. Note the arrondissement of each shop, as some have several branches.

A few questions

What is the most famous dessert in Lyon?

Tarte aux pralines, a tart filled with melted pink pralines.

What is a coussin de Lyon?

A small green, cushion-shaped sweet of chocolate and marzipan.

Do chocolatiers in Lyon have seating?

Not always. Many are shops only, so check before planning a sit-down stop.
