The best cafés in Busan for travelers

Busan's café culture runs from dense backstreets to cliff-top spaces with ocean views. Choose one area per afternoon and the city feels easy.

Published August 21, 2026 · Updated September 5, 2026 · 3 minutes

* * *

Jeonpo

Jeonpo's side streets, near Seomyeon, are packed with specialty coffee shops, bakeries and small dessert cafés. It is the easiest place for a first café afternoon, because so much is within a few blocks.

Gwangalli

Facing Gwangan Bridge, Gwangalli's beach streets have coffee and dessert stops with water views. Late afternoon and evening, when the bridge lights up, are popular times.

Haeundae and Haeridan-gil

Around Haeundae, there is a mix of polished cafés, bakeries and trendy desserts, with Haeridan-gil offering smaller independent spots close to the station.

Yeongdo and Gijang

Further out, Yeongdo island and the Gijang coast have larger cafés with sea views and more space. Worth the trip when you want a long, unhurried sit.

What to order

Korean cafés are known for creative desserts: bingsu (shaved ice with toppings), elaborate cakes and pastries, and seasonal drinks. In Busan, try ssiat hotteok, a seed-filled sweet pancake from street stalls, as a snack between cafés.

Some small cafés close one day a week. Check before making a special trip.

When to go

Weekday mornings are calmest. Jeonpo gets livelier from lunchtime; Gwangalli and Haeundae peak from mid-afternoon into sunset, and sea-view cafés can have queues at sunset itself.

What it costs

Before publication, coffee was roughly ₩4,000 to ₩7,000 and a plated dessert or larger bakery item ₩6,000 to ₩12,000. Ocean-view cafés were often higher.

Finding cafés

Naver Map and KakaoMap have more complete café listings in Korea than international map apps. Save the Korean place name and the nearest subway exit before leaving Wi-Fi.

A few questions

Where is Busan's café street?

Jeonpo, near Seomyeon, has a dense concentration of cafés and bakeries.

Where can I find a sea-view café in Busan?

Gwangalli, Haeundae, Yeongdo and the Gijang coast all have cafés with ocean views.

Which map app is best for finding cafés in Busan?

Naver Map or KakaoMap, which have more complete local listings.
